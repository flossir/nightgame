Nightgame (2026)

When two friends discover the archived streams of an obscure pickup artist/lolcow named Matt Nightgame, they spend a kratom fueled night watching his videos for laughs. Weeks later they recognize one of his targets in a post about a missing woman and realize the videos may actually contain evidence of a terrible crime.
